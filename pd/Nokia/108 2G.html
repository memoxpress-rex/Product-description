One solid look

The stylish streak that runs across every edge of the phone seems like it’s just a design choice, when in fact, it’s actually a solid inner frame that gives it some real toughness.

Talk when you want, for as long as you want.

The longer they last, the better. Nokia 108 has a 1000 mAh removable battery that lasts for up to 18 days on standby.

Work hard, play harder.

On the practical side of things, there’s a voice recorder,a high power flashlight, and room for up to 2000 contacts. But here’s the fun part: there’s an FM radio, MP3 player, and of course, the Snake game. What more do you need?
