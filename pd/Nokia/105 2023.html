Bigger battery

Nokia 105 features a 25% bigger battery than the previous model, giving you up to 22 days of standby and up to 12 hours of talk time per charge. Top up with a standard Micro USB cable.

Keep your contacts close

With space for up to 2,000 contacts and 500 SMS messages, there’s nothing to stop you staying in touch.

Local music and news at your fingertips.

Listen to news on your morning commute or play music at home. The wireless FM radio works with or without a headset, so you’re good to go.
